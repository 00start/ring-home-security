## Key Decisions Made

1. **Default buffering to OFF** - Major battery saving, can be enabled for high-security zones
2. **30-second polling interval** - Balance between responsiveness and battery life
3. **20% battery threshold** - Pause streaming before critical level
4. **5-minute live view timeout** - Prevent forgotten streams draining battery
5. **30-second recording duration** - Shorter than 60s default for battery savings
